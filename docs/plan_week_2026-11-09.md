## Go / no-go for putting a module in the trial
- ASR: not worse than I2 on benchmark v2 short (zh / zh-en / en each, paired bootstrap) and long; p95 latency < 0.5 s for
  clips < 15 s on vLLM. Otherwise the trial uses I2 (Whisper) for ASR.
- Post-processing: on pp_eval_v0 at least as good as the online teacher on every phenomenon the owner cares about,
  hallucination / answering rate ≤ teacher's, p95 < 200 ms for typical inputs. Otherwise the trial shows raw ASR.

## Risks
- Review time (E1 + E2 + E3 + P3 ≈ 8–10 h of owner time this week) — the plan slips if reviews slip; order of
  priority: E1 > P3 > E2 > E3.
- GPU queue on the shared cluster; vLLM 0.14 + CUDA 13 driver compatibility (unverified).
- Chinese post-processing data is scarce (≈ 4% of traffic) unless P1 approves generation.
- A serving host for real users is outside this week's scope (security, uptime, scaling).
